Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 September 2026

1. Introduction

LinkShield Sandbox ("we", "our", "the app") is a privacy-first secure browser that opens every link in an isolated sandbox environment. This policy explains how we handle your data.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

LinkShield Sandbox is designed with privacy as its core principle. We do not collect, store, or share the following:

  • Browsing history
  • Passwords or form data
  • Cookies or session information
  • Personal information (name, email, phone)
  • Location data
  • Contact lists
  • Device identifiers for tracking

3. Data We Do Process

3.1 URL Threat Checking

When you open a URL, we send it to these threat intelligence services for security verification:

  • Google Safe Browsing API
  • URLhaus (abuse.ch)
  • AlienVault OTX
  • ThreatFox (abuse.ch)
  • PhishStats

These services check if the URL is malicious. We do not store these URLs on our servers.

3.2 Media Downloads (Grabber)

When you use the Media Grabber feature, the URL is sent to our self-hosted server (yt-dlp / Cobalt) which returns the media file. The file is delivered directly to your device's Downloads folder.

3.3 License Validation (Pro Users)

If you activate Pro, your license key is validated against a public list of SHA-256 hashes hosted on GitHub. Your key itself is never transmitted or stored.

4. Data Storage

  • All browsing data is deleted when you close the app
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs are used
  • No advertising networks
  • No third-party data sharing

5. Permissions We Request

  • INTERNET: Required for browsing and threat checking
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: For detecting network connectivity
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: For download progress notifications
  • CAMERA: For QR code scanning (optional)
  • WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE: For saving media files (Android 9 and below)

6. Third-Party Services

Our app interacts with these third-party services:

  • Google Safe Browsing (Google LLC)
  • URLhaus & ThreatFox (abuse.ch)
  • AlienVault OTX (LevelBlue)
  • GitHub (Microsoft) — for license validation

7. Children's Privacy

LinkShield Sandbox does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. The app is safe for all ages as it does not collect personal data.

We may update this policy. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
